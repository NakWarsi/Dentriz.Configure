Meet the Top Dentists in Roadpali & Kalamboli

At ClinSmile Dental Clinic, we believe every smile deserves to shine! Our expert dentists in Navi Mumbai provide complete preventive, restorative, and cosmetic care - from dental implants in Roadpali and cosmetic dentistry to trusted family dentistry - all under one roof with comfort and care you can trust.

Dr. Akash Mankar, BDS

Founder and Chief Dentist

Dr. Akash Mankar is a highly skilled dental professional with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) degree from Government Dental College, Mumbai — one of India's premier dental institutions.

She has over 8 years of clinical experience in dentistry, including 3 years of service as a government dentist at GDC Mumbai, where she gained extensive expertise in treating a wide range of dental conditions.

Dr. Rizwana specializes in cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign treatment, and comprehensive family care. Known for her gentle approach, clear communication, and attention to detail, she ensures that every patient feels comfortable and confident throughout their treatment journey.
