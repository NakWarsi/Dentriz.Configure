Tips for Parents

Helpful advice to maintain your child's oral health at home

💡

Start dental visits by age 1 or when first tooth appears

💡

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste

💡

Limit sugary snacks and drinks

💡

Use dental sealants for cavity prevention

💡

Encourage regular flossing habits

💡

Schedule checkups every 6 months

💡

Consider mouthguards for sports activities

💡

Lead by example with good oral hygiene

Preparing for Your Child's First Visit

Make the first dental visit a positive experience

Before the Visit

  • Read books about dental visits
  • Role-play with a toothbrush
  • Explain what to expect
  • Choose a comfortable time

During the Visit

  • Stay calm and positive
  • Let the dentist lead
  • Provide comfort if needed
  • Ask questions

After the Visit

  • Praise good behavior
  • Discuss the experience
  • Schedule follow-up
  • Maintain good habits
