Professional Whitening Options

Choose the whitening treatment that's right for you.

💡

In-Office Whitening

Professional whitening treatment performed in our office for immediate results.

Duration: 1-2 hours
Results: 3-8 shades brighter
  • Immediate results
  • Professional supervision
  • Customized treatment
  • Safe and effective
  • Long-lasting results
🏠

Take-Home Whitening

Custom-fitted trays with professional whitening gel for at-home treatment.

Duration: 2-4 weeks
Results: 2-6 shades brighter
  • Custom-fitted trays
  • Professional whitening gel
  • Convenient at-home use
  • Gradual whitening
  • Maintenance treatment
🌟

Combination Treatment

Best results with in-office treatment followed by take-home maintenance.

Duration: 1-2 hours + 2 weeks
Results: 5-10 shades brighter
  • Immediate in-office results
  • Take-home maintenance
  • Longest-lasting results
  • Customized treatment plan
  • Professional guidance
