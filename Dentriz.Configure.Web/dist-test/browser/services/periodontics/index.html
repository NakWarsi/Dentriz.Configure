Warning Signs of Gum Disease

Don't ignore these symptoms - early detection is key to successful treatment

⚠️

Bleeding gums during brushing or flossing

⚠️

Red, swollen, or tender gums

⚠️

Persistent bad breath

⚠️

Receding gums or longer appearing teeth

⚠️

Loose or shifting teeth

⚠️

Pain when chewing

⚠️

Sensitive teeth

Experiencing Any of These Symptoms?

Contact us immediately for a comprehensive periodontal evaluation. Early treatment can prevent serious complications.
