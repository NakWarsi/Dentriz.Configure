Emergency Dental Services

We provide immediate care for all types of dental emergencies.

🦷

Toothache Relief

Immediate pain relief for severe toothaches and dental pain.

Common Symptoms:

  • Severe tooth pain
  • Sensitivity to hot/cold
  • Pain when chewing
  • Swelling around tooth
  • Throbbing pain
🔧

Broken Tooth Repair

Emergency repair for chipped, cracked, or broken teeth.

Common Symptoms:

  • Visible tooth damage
  • Sharp edges
  • Pain when biting
  • Sensitivity to air
  • Bleeding from tooth
👑

Lost Filling/Crown

Emergency replacement of lost or damaged dental restorations.

Common Symptoms:

  • Missing filling or crown
  • Sharp edges
  • Sensitivity to temperature
  • Pain when eating
  • Visible tooth damage
⚡

Emergency Extractions

Urgent tooth removal for severely damaged or infected teeth.

Common Symptoms:

  • Severe infection
  • Unbearable pain
  • Swelling in face/jaw
  • Fever
  • Difficulty breathing
🚑

Dental Trauma

Emergency care for sports injuries, accidents, and dental trauma.

Common Symptoms:

  • Knocked-out tooth
  • Bleeding from mouth
  • Jaw pain
  • Loose teeth
  • Facial swelling

1

Emergency Care Tips

🦷

Knocked-Out Tooth

Keep the tooth moist, don't touch the root, and get to our office within 30 minutes.

🔧

Broken Tooth

Rinse with warm water, apply cold compress, and save any broken pieces.

😣

Severe Pain

Take over-the-counter pain reliever and contact us immediately.

👑

Lost Crown/Filling

Keep the restoration safe and avoid chewing on that side until repaired.
